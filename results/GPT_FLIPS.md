## Classification scheme

Each Arm B reason is assigned one of:

- **(a)** Explicitly notes a specific methodological difference visible in the descriptions, then argues it will not cause a large potency shift  
- **(b)** Asserts similarity (same target, same format, same organism) without engaging with differences present in the descriptions or flagged by Arm A  
- **(c)** Other

**Implication for the mechanism hypothesis in FINDINGS.md**

The current claim: *"same target plus similar description reads as confirmation of poolability."*
